Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 January 2026

Dev With Lari ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed by us, including:

  • ImageTap – Image to PDF, PDF Tools & Scanner
  • Best QR Code Scanner – QR & Barcode Scanner

Information Collection and Use

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information on our servers.

All processing is performed locally on your device only.

ImageTap – Data Handling

ImageTap provides tools such as:

  • Resize images
  • Add watermark
  • Convert images
  • Compress images
  • JPG to PDF
  • PDF to Image
  • Merge & Split PDF
  • PDF to Word
  • PDF Compression
  • Image to Text (OCR)

Local Processing

All images, documents, and PDFs are processed locally on your device. Files are never uploaded to our servers.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Created or converted files
  • Compressed PDFs
  • App settings

Best QR Code Scanner – Data Handling

All QR and barcode scans are processed locally.

  • Scan history
  • Favorite items
  • Generated codes

This data stays only on your device unless deleted manually.

Permissions Used

Camera Permission

Used only for:

  • Scanning QR/Barcodes
  • Capturing images for OCR

No photos or videos are recorded or transmitted.

Storage Permission

Used to:

  • Save created files
  • Open selected images or PDFs
  • Export or share documents

Internet Permission

Used only for advertisements and purchase verification.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The apps display ads provided by Google AdMob.

  • Device information
  • Advertising ID
  • Approximate location (non-precise)

Google handles this data according to their policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

ImageTap offers optional premium features through a one-time purchase. Payments are securely processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store payment information.

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third party.

Children’s Privacy

Our apps are not specifically directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect children’s data.

Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, you maintain full control of your information.
